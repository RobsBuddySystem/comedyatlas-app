Connect Instagram.

A guide for show runners. Last updated 8 November 2026.

What the integration does

  • Schedule feed posts and stories for your shows from Comedy Atlas.
  • Automatically invite the venue and the comics on the bill as Instagram Collaborators, and add tags.
  • Show you reach and click stats for the posts we publish for you.

Status: the connection is in Meta app review. Until it is approved, you can prepare your account using the steps below.

Step by step

  1. Switch Instagram to a Professional account. In the Instagram app: Settings and privacy → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account. Choose Creator or Business (either works).
  2. Optional: link a Facebook Page. In Instagram: Edit profile → Links / Page → Connect or create a Facebook Page. Some features need the Page link; we will tell you if yours does.
  3. Make sure you can approve collaborations. In Instagram settings, allow tags and collaborator invitations from the venue and comics you work with.
  4. Connect Instagram on Comedy Atlas. Sign in to your show-runner account and press the button below. You will approve the permissions on Meta's own screen; we never see your Instagram password.
  5. Schedule a post. Pick a show, write or generate the caption, choose the venue and comics to invite as collaborators, and set the time.

What data we access

  • Your account id and username, an access token, the media we publish for you, and insights for your own posts.
  • Used only to provide this service to you; never sold and never used for other clients. Tokens are stored encrypted.

Details: Privacy policy.

How to disconnect

Remove "Comedy Atlas Social" in your Instagram or Facebook settings, or email us. Step-by-step instructions are on the Data deletion page. We delete your token and connection data within 30 days.
