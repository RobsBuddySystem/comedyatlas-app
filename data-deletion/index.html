Data deletion.

Instructions for removing your data and the Facebook / Instagram connection. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Option 1: remove the app in Facebook or Instagram

  1. Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations), select "Comedy Atlas Social", then Remove.
  2. Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites (or Accounts Center → Apps and websites), select "Comedy Atlas Social", then Remove.
  3. Removing the app revokes our access immediately. We then delete your access token and the data linked to the connection within 30 days.

Option 2: ask us by email

Email contact@comedyatlas.app with the subject "Data deletion". Include the email address of your account and, if relevant, your Instagram or Facebook username so we can find your records. We reply with a confirmation code.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and sign-in data.
  • Your Facebook / Instagram connection: account id, username, access tokens, and any insights or media metadata stored for you.
  • Scheduled posts and drafts you created with our tools.
  • Messages and form submissions you sent us, unless the law requires us to keep a record.

Anonymous aggregate statistics cannot identify you and may be kept. Security logs rotate automatically.

How long it takes

We complete deletion within 30 days of your request, or of you removing the app, and confirm by email.

Check the status

Reply to our message quoting your confirmation code, or email contact@comedyatlas.app with the code, and we will tell you the current status of your request.
