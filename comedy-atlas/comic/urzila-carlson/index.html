Urzila Carlson.

Comic

Urzila Carlson is a South African-born New Zealand comedian and actress, known for her stand-up performances as well as her appearances on television programs in both New Zealand and Australia. Carlson is a regular panellist on 7 Days and both the Australian and New Zealand version of Have You Been Paying Attention? She was also a panellist on The Masked Singer Australia for the show's second and third seasons and a contestant on the both the second season of Taskmaster New Zealand and sixth season of Taskmaster Australia. Urzila also played Fallon in the comedy film Kinda Pregnant alongside Amy Schumer and Brianne Howey.
