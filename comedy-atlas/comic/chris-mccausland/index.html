Chris McCausland.

Comic

Christopher John McCausland is an English actor and comedian. He is known to television audiences for his role as Rudi in the CBeebies show Me Too!. He regularly appears at comedy venues including The Comedy Store. McCausland is blind due to retinitis pigmentosa.
