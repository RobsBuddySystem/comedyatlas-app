Ali Siddiq.

Comic

Siddiq Nassir Abdullah Ali, known professionally as Ali Siddiq, is an American stand-up comedian, writer and former radio personality based in Houston, Texas. He debuted his comedy album in 2010, his half-hour television special in 2016 and his hour-long TV special in 2018. Siddiq was the winner of Comedy Central's inaugural Up Next competition in 2013 and was a finalist on NBC's competition TV show Bring the Funny in 2019. He was the co-host of R&B afternoon radio show Uncle Funky Larry Jones & Ali Siddiq on KMJQ in Greater Houston from January 2021 to January 2024.
