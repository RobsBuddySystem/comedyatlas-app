Who is it for?

For anyone who organises team life: HR, internal communications, office managers, event teams and works councils (CSE). An hour of comedy is a shared moment: people laugh together, and teams talk to each other differently the next day.

The show is in English, so it suits international teams or teams used to working in English. A bilingual EN/FR version is possible.

For French works councils (CSE)

Two ways to offer comedy to employees: a dedicated show for a CSE event (the year-end party, for example), or an outing to French Fried Comedy Night for employees and their guests.

We can send a one-page proposal to present to your committee. If you are not the right person, simply pass it on to the culture or social-activities (ASC) committee.

Year-end parties get planned in the autumn: the earlier you write, the more dates we can offer.

Frequently asked questions

Is the show in French?

Our shows are in English, with a bilingual EN/FR version possible. If you are looking for a fully French-language show, write to us: we will tell you honestly what we can offer.

How many people is it for?

From 20 to 100 people for a show at your place. For a smaller or larger group, write to us.

How long is the show?

45 to 60 minutes.

What equipment do we need?

A microphone is enough. Sound needs are confirmed with you at booking.

Is the content suitable for a professional audience?

Tell us the context in your request (team, atmosphere, topics to avoid): we take it into account when choosing the comedians.

How much notice is needed?

For the evening mentioned above, three weeks passed between the first message and the show. Write to us with your preferred period and we will reply with the dates that are possible.

How do we get a proposal?

Write to us with the date or period, the place and the number of people. We prepare a tailored proposal, sent on request.
