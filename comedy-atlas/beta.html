The offer

You get every paid feature that exists at the time of your enrollment, free, for the lifetime of your account, in exchange for actively testing COMEDY ATLAS and giving feedback during the beta.

  • "Lifetime" means the lifetime of your account on COMEDY ATLAS, while the service operates.

  • Your account must remain active and within the terms of use.
  • Recorded: your enrollment date and the scope of the offer are written to our records when you redeem, so the promise survives any future pricing change.
  • Limited to 20 founding testers.

The honest note

We show this on purpose: today, most of COMEDY ATLAS is free for everyone and paid features barely exist. What you're really getting is a permanent grandfather into whatever premium becomes — plus our attention while you test.
