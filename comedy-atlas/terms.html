Content, intellectual property & takedowns

This is the general route for reporting a copyright, ownership, or accuracy problem with anything on the site — a photo, a bio, scraped listing text, or a claim made on someone else's behalf. It's separate from the comic-listing removal path described in our Privacy page, which covers a comic asking for their own personal listing to be changed or taken down.

  • Where content comes from. Show, venue, and listing text is aggregated from official venue websites, festival programmes, and public ticketing platforms, and cross-checked against OpenStreetMap for locations. Comic and booker photos are either uploaded by the account holder or, where a public photo is used to identify a public figure for a listing, sourced from a public page. We don't have a formal photo-licensing programme — if you believe a photo on the site is yours and shouldn't be there, tell us and we'll take it down while we sort it out.
  • If you believe your work, photo, or likeness is on the site without a right to be there — as the rights holder or someone authorized to act for them — email contact@comedyatlas.app with: (1) what the content is and where it appears (a URL), (2) a description of your right to it, and (3) how to reach you. A human reads every takedown request.
  • What happens next. We aim to remove or update disputed content promptly while we look into it — we would rather take something down and restore it once we've verified the facts than leave a disputed claim live. This is a manual process run by one person, not an automated notice-and-counter-notice system, so response times can vary.
  • If a listing misattributes a bit, a booking, or a credit to the wrong comic or venue, that's an accuracy report, not strictly an IP claim — send it to the same address and we'll correct it.
  • We don't claim ownership over your bio, photos, or any content you submit for your own listing. You keep it; you're granting us permission to display it on COMEDY ATLAS for as long as your listing is live, or until you ask us to remove it.
