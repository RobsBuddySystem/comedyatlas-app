Take the tour

  1. Fans: find a show

    Browse the world map, jump to any city page, or open the shows calendar. Every listing links to the organiser's own ticket page.

    Find a show

    Festival programmes sit next to the shows and venues around them. Festival organisers can be listed too.

    See festivals

    Every comic gets a page with bio, links and upcoming shows. Claiming one is free.

    Claim your page

    Read the Handbook

    Open mics are where every comic starts. This guide shows how to find the ones near you.

    Find an open mic

    Once you are on stage, apply directly to the people who book the shows.

    Apply for a spot

    The booker portal is where you build line-ups and invite comics to them. Venue deal terms are set in the same place.

    Open the booker portal

    From club comics to bigger names, tell us the date, the room and the budget. We reply by email.

    Email us

  9. What's coming: the comic's appComing

    One place for a comic's gigs, bookings and tour dates. It is not built yet; tell us what you would want in it.

    Tell us what you'd want
