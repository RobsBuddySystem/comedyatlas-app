COMEDY ATLAS is a working directory of upcoming stand-up shows, comics, and bookers in English-speaking comedy scenes worldwide. The people using it are industry, not casual browsers: comics checking stage-time opportunities, bookers filling lineups, and venues managing their own listings — today concentrated in eight active markets by listing volume (Montreal, London, Edinburgh, Barcelona, Madrid, Amsterdam, Toronto, Berlin), with lighter coverage growing across 37 cities that already have at least one live listing.

The quality proof

78.4%
of every live listing on the site was approved at our system's own high-confidence bar: independently corroborated by a second source, or sourced directly from an official venue/promoter/festival page or a ticket platform — the same threshold our pipeline requires before a listing is allowed to publish without a human reviewing it first. The other 21.6% still went through the identical scoring and approval pipeline, just at a lower confidence score. Every one of our 5,120 live listings — 100% — has a traceable approval record: no listing on this site appeared from nowhere.

That's not a vanity metric — it's the real output of a table-driven confidence-scoring model, checked against the live database the same day this page was written. This number moves as the pipeline runs — it read 78.3% (3,997/5,106) about five minutes earlier this same session, and 59.3% a few hours before that, purely from ordinary ingest activity, not a methodology change. Full methodology and the exact SQL are documented internally (docs/atlas-ops/PRECISION_2026-07-25.md) and available on request.
