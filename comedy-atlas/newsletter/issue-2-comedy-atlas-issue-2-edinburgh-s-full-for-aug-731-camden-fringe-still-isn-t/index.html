COMEDY ATLAS — Issue 2: Edinburgh's full for Aug 7–31, Camden Fringe still isn't

2026-07-23 · COMEDY ATLAS

Edinburgh Fringe's 2026 programme just locked at 3,600+ shows for its August 7–31 run — and registration closed weeks ago. If you're not already in that number, the wave crested without you.

But August isn't fully booked. Here's what to do this week before one city swallows the whole calendar.

Book a room while there's still lead time.

The Comedy Nest in Montreal runs a weekly English open mic, Newbie Tuesday — 5-minute sets, no crowd work, on Rue Sainte-Catherine Ouest (Montreal Forum). They book 3–5 weeks out, so book through comedynest.com/open-mic this week if you want a slot before summer's out.

Madrid Comedy Lab calls itself the city's only dedicated English-standup club — check their Upcoming Shows page for the current mic schedule.

In Dublin, The International Bar has run comedy most nights since 1988, including a Wednesday "Comedy Cellar" — worth a look if you're passing through, though mic details aren't nailed down yet.

The door that's still open: Camden Fringe.

Aug 3–30, 39 venues, 440+ shows booked — and they're still taking acts through Eventotron. No published cutoff means the door's closing, not closed. If Edinburgh passed you by, this is the move.

Why the guy at the stadium matters to you at the mic.

Shane Gillis sold more than 76,000 tickets to one show at Lincoln Financial Field on July 17 — a Guinness World Record for a solo comedian. That's not a "someday" stat; it's a ceiling that just moved. The five minutes you're building this month and that stadium gig are the same craft at different scale — remember that on the nights the room is twelve people deep.

One to watch, not act on yet:

Leicester Comedy Festival filed a serious incident report with the UK Charity Commission over financial difficulties. Its 2027 edition still plans to open registrations in late August — but don't treat that date as routine. Wait for confirmation before building plans around it.

Book through comedynest.com/open-mic today — Camden Fringe can wait a week, Montreal cannot.
