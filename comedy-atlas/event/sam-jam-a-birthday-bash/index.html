Sam Jam (A Birthday Bash)!.

Wed, Sep 2 2026 · 19:00 (UTC-04:00)

This guy's b-day falls on a Wed this year! We thought, "What the heck, why not let him take over  $5 Improv Night  to celebrate?" GREAT IF YOU ENJOY... Dropout TV, Ben Schwartz & Friends SHOW : Wed, Sep 2nd @ 7pm TICKETS : $5 (free for students) To purchase tix, select quantity in upper right & click "Add to Cart." No physical tix will be mailed; simply bring your ID the night of the performance. Want to use a gift card? Please email , call ([contact removed]), or visit the Box Office to redeem. ABOUT THE SHOW Sam Kurnit  is a long-time member of the Vermont comedy community. You'll recognize him from improv shows, standup shows, working in the box office and sound booth, teaching, and even being the voice of our pre-show announcement! He's also the Director of Education for Vermont Comedy Arts , and a pun enthusiast. We're celebrating Sam turning 42 by letting him take the reins for one night only. We don't know exactly what he has planned, but we do know that it involves YOU! All he's told us is that the whole show will be a jam that anyone can play in . So come play and say happy birthday!
