COMEDY FOR A CAUSE: AZK9 RESCUE.

Sun, Sep 13 2026 · 18:00 (UTC-07:00)

Rand Comedy Productions produces various shows throughout Southern California, Arizona & Texas, including Comedy for a Cause. Every Comedy for a Cause show is produced in partnership with a local charitable organization that receives a portion of ticket sales, after expenses. Come enjoy a good laugh while helping a great organization. AZK9 Rescue is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving Arizona’s underserved communities through our three-fold mission of education, sterilization, and rescue. Founded 16 years ago by a group of friends and former shelter volunteers, we began with a simple goal: to make a difference for companion animals and the people who love them. Since then, we have provided nearly 20,000 free spay and neuter surgeries for community pets and rescued more than 2,500 dogs and cats, including animals who have been abused, abandoned, overlooked, or considered difficult to place. While we are an all-breed rescue, our work often focuses on Bully breeds, Chihuahuas, seniors, and animals with significant medical needs. We believe every life is worth saving and no animal is a lost cause. Through medical care, training, socialization, and plenty of love, we work to give each animal in our care the second chance they deserve. For 16 years, our community of volunteers, partners, donors, adopters, and supporters has helped us continue this work, and we are honored to keep showing up for the animals who need us most.
