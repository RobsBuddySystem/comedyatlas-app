CHASE HARTER.

Wed, Oct 14 2026 · 19:30 (UTC-07:00)

Whether it be through a jacket, or the words out his mouth, Chase Harter is always making a statement. Chase was raised in Phoenix Arizona, but thankfully was educated elsewhere growing up, and has since honed his craft through a slew of fast paced clever wordplay, stacking punchline after punchline. His comedy is heavily inspired and powered by his untreated ADHD and his tendency towards run-on sentences; You never know what he is going to say next, and likely neither does he, but you can count on it being erratic but hilarious. Chase touches on any topic through his lens as a gay man with raging ADHD traversing a red state trying to bring everyone together. Don’t worry, if the gay part scares you, he’ll walk you to your car. You may have already incidentally seen this man, as he’s a regular opener for such legends as Amber Autry, Fiona Cauley, and Heather Shaw. Regardless, Chase has a made a name for himself through showcasing through the multiple festivals and competitions. Chase has showcased through Columbus, Big Pine, and West End comedy festivals, all sparking his competitive spirit, and now holds the titles of “Funny Phoenix 2026” and “Funniest Comedian in the Universe 2025” while being a finalist in “Funniest comedian with a day job 2024” then getting 3 different Best of Fest awards from the Big Pine Festival.
