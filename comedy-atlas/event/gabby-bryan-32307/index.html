The hilarious GABBY BRYAN returns to VT! GREAT IF YOU ENJOY... You've seen her on Amazon and HBO; now see her LIVE! SHOWS : Fri, April 16th @ 7pm & 9pm ($30) Sat, April 17th @ 7pm & 9pm ($30) To purchase tix, select quantity in upper right & click "Add to Cart." No physical tix will be mailed; simply bring your ID the night of the performance. Want to use a gift card? Please email , call ([contact removed]), or visit the Box Office to redeem. ABOUT THE COMEDIAN GABBY BRYAN is a New York-based stand-up comedian, actress, Emmy-award winning producer, and overall girlie girl. Currently performing across the US and UK to sold out crowds with her tour Gabby Bryan is My Best Friend. Gabby recently brought her Fringe hit Billy and the Situation to Union Hall, garnering praise in the NYT. Her crowd work special Girl Town Hall is currently streaming on YouTube. On the film side, she performed alongside Olivia Wilde and Oscar Isaac in “Life Itself” (Amazon Prime). Gabby co-hosts "L'Podcast" with comedian Zack Signore, which has built a global audience, as well as "Let Me Ask My Dad” Podcast with musician and composer David Bryan. She is wildly popular on social media with over 500K followers, sharing viral stand-up clips & comedy sketches. Follow her at [handle removed] on all platforms! CHECK HER OUT!
