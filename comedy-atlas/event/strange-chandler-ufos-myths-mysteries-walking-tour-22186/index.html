STRANGE CHANDLER - UFOs, MYTHS & MYSTERIES WALKING TOUR.

Sun, Sep 6 2026 · 20:30 (UTC-07:00)

🛸 UFOs, Myths & Mysteries: Strange Chandler A Walking Tour Through the Weird, the Wild, and the Unexplained Chandler may look quiet under the desert sky, but history suggests we’re not always alone. UFOs, Myths & Mysteries: Strange Chandler is a guided walking tour exploring the city’s strangest stories: unexplained lights, lost time, electromagnetic anomalies, Indigenous sky legends, and real incidents documented in police logs, maintenance records, and eyewitness accounts. From the night of the 1997 Phoenix Lights , when clocks reportedly stopped downtown, to shimmering fountains, glowing alleyways, sky serpents, and modern light installations that may be repeating old signals, this tour blends documented events, desert science, folklore, and pop-culture mystery into one unforgettable experience. This isn’t a haunted house, and it’s not a lecture. It’s a playful, eerie journey through Chandler’s most curious moments, told with humor, history, and just enough “what if?” to keep you looking up. You’ll walk the same streets where: witnesses reported hovering craft and power surges water rippled without wind time seemed to skip lights appeared, vanished, and sometimes came back Whether you believe these stories are extraterrestrial, scientific, psychological, or something else entirely … Strange Chandler invites you to decide for yourself. 👣 Tour Details 📍 Start & End: Mic Drop Comedy, 51 E Boston St 🚶 Distance: ~½ mile loop ⏰ Duration: ~50 minutes 👟 Please wear comfortable walking shoes 👽 Recommended for teens & adults Perfect for locals, visitors, skeptics, sci-fi fans, and anyone who enjoys history with a strange twist. Some towns have ghost stories. Chandler has sightings. Come walk beneath the lights, and see what notices you back. No refunds or exchanges.
