Standup Open Mic.

Wed, Aug 19 2026 · 20:30 (UTC-04:00)

Presented by Vermont Comedy Arts ; the popular standup comedy open mic happens every Wed! SHOW: 8:30pm - 10pm ADMISSION : FREE! (no advance tix needed) AGES : ALL (18+ recommended; adult content) ABOUT THE OPEN MIC Our weekly standup open mic at Vermont Comedy Club has been a popular Wednesday tradition for years. Come throw your name in the hat and tell some jokes -- or just come to watch, laugh and cheer people on. FREE! SIGNUP TIME: 8:00pm* SIGNUP LOCATION : Box Office MIC DETAILS : 3 mins; random order; no hate speech * We ask performers to line up at the Box Office in the order they arrived at the club/not to rush to the front of the line. We always have an "overflow list" for comedians who didn't get on the main list, in case there is time at the end for more performers. Please be kind and fair to staff and other comedians!
